On the planet Winter, the Handdarata is one of the two major religions, the other one being the Yomeshta. The cornerstone belief of the Handdarata is that one should strive towards ignorance, while the Yomeshta hold that all of time was seen at once by Meshe, and so all is already known, all is light. Despite the pursuit of ignorance, the Handdarata follow the mystical ritual of foretelling. The ritual has primarily the asker and the foretellers: the asker gets to ask one question, usually at a high material cost and often an even higher personal cost. It seems funny that the religion of ignorance has the ability to foretell the future so exactly. The foretellings seldom bring the solace the asker sought; rather, their lives are further worsened by knowing than by unknowing. It goes to show the value of ignorance to human existence, and the futility of chasing certainty, for a life fully known is death.

Ignorance is a ground of thought. Unproof is the ground of action.
